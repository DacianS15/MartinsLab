## Research Themes

The Martin Research Lab investigates the cognitive, neural, and social mechanisms underlying human behaviour across healthy and clinical populations.

### Social Cognition and Theory of Mind

Our research explores how people understand the thoughts, intentions, emotions, and perspectives of others. This includes perspective-taking, theory of mind, empathy, and social decision-making.

### Sense of Agency and the Self

We investigate how individuals experience control over their actions and how self-referential processing influences memory, cognition, and behaviour.

### Executive Functions

Research in this area examines cognitive control, inhibition, initiation, planning, and decision-making across healthy and clinical populations.

### Social Neuroscience

Using behavioural, neuropsychological, neuroimaging, and brain stimulation methods, we investigate the neural mechanisms supporting social cognition and interpersonal behaviour.

### Psychosis and Mental Health

The lab examines cognitive and social cognitive processes associated with psychosis, schizophrenia, and related mental health difficulties.

### Loneliness and Social Connection

We investigate how loneliness influences memory, social cognition, mental health, and wellbeing across the lifespan.

### Ageing and Neuropsychology

Our research explores cognitive ageing, age-related changes in social cognition, and interventions designed to support healthy cognitive functioning in later life.

## Research Methods

- Clinical Research
- Behavioural Science
